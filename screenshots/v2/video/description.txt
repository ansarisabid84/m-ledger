✨ WHAT YOU'LL LEARN
• Set up Ledger in under a minute and choose expenses-only or income + expenses
• See how much you can safely spend today and your month-end forecast
• Log an expense in seconds with category tiles and quick-add chips
• Find anything with search, filters and the heat-map spending calendar
• Swipe a transaction to edit or delete it; swipe to change month
• Reorder and hide dashboard widgets to design your own Home screen
• Track savings goals, money lent and borrowed (with part payments) and recurring bills
• Protect your data with app lock, hide-amounts, and automatic backups
