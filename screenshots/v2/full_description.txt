Ledger is a beautiful, private expense tracker that works completely offline. No account, no ads, no cloud — your money data stays on your device.

Track what you spend, stay inside your budget and always know how much you can safely spend today.

KNOW WHAT YOU CAN SPEND TODAY
• Daily allowance based on your monthly budget and the days left
• Month-end forecast that warns you before you overspend
• Category budgets with alerts at 80% and 100%
• Spending streaks to build a daily logging habit

LOG AN EXPENSE IN SECONDS
• Big amount field, one-tap category tiles and payment methods (UPI, cards, cash, net banking, wallet)
• Quick-add chips for the expenses you repeat most
• Log in any currency with automatic conversion
• Paste a bank SMS to pre-fill a transaction (optional)
• Undo a delete instantly

SEE WHERE YOUR MONEY GOES
• Heat-map spending calendar — tap a day to filter
• Search and filter by category, method or note
• Swipe a transaction to edit or delete
• Charts by category, payment method and 6-month trend
• Swipe left or right to change month, everywhere

YOUR DASHBOARD, YOUR WAY
• Reorder, hide or show widgets to design your own Home screen
• Expenses-only mode: turn income tracking off for a simple, focused ledger — or keep full income, balance and month-end settlements

PLAN AHEAD
• Bills & subscriptions: every recurring payment in one place — pause, resume or stop
• Savings goals with progress, deadlines and contributions
• Borrowed & lent tracker with due dates and part payments
• Smart reminders: daily logging nudge, debt follow-ups and custom alerts

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• App lock with fingerprint / Face ID or your own PIN
• One-tap hide-amounts toggle
• 100% offline — no sign-up, no tracking

SAFE BACKUPS
• Full JSON backup and restore (merge or replace)
• Automatic scheduled backups to your device
• Export transactions to CSV or JSON
